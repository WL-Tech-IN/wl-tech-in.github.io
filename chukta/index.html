How It Works

Settling Debts in 3 Simple Steps

No complicated accounting. From start to finish, Chukta is designed to save you time.

Step 1

Create a Group or Duo

Start a group for your apartment, weekend trip, dinner outing, or simply maintain a running 1-on-1 tab with your partner or flatmate.

Step 2

Add Expenses in Seconds

Enter the amount, select who paid and who participated. Chukta calculates each person's exact share automatically.

Step 3

Hit "Chukta" to Settle Up

When it's time to clear balances, tap Settle. Pay via UPI or cash, and mark the ledger Chukta with one tap.
