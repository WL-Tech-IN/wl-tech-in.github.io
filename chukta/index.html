Designed for Simplicity

Everything You Need to Keep Finances Clear

Chukta turns messy group expenses and complicated calculations into a breeze with intuitive features crafted for everyday use.

Smart Group Splitting

Split equally, by exact amount, percentage, or custom itemized bills. Perfect for flatmates, couples, dinner outings, and multi-day vacations.

1-Tap "Chukta" Settlements

Instantly settle balances with built-in deep links to your favorite payment apps (UPI, GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or Net Banking). One tap and debts are cleared.

Offline-First Architecture

Trekking in remote hills or traveling abroad with no cell reception? Chukta works completely offline and syncs seamlessly the second you reconnect.

Multi-Currency Support

Traveling internationally? Record expenses in any local currency (USD, EUR, GBP, AED, THB) with auto-converted exchange rates for easy reconciliation.

Private & Ad-Free

No annoying banner ads, no targeted behavioral tracking, and no selling your financial history. Your records are completely private to your groups.

Smart Export & Summaries

Generate clean PDF breakdowns or CSV exports of your monthly expenses or completed trips for personal records and expense reimbursements.

How It Works

Settling Debts in 3 Simple Steps

No steep learning curve. From start to finish, Chukta is designed to save you time.

Step 1

Create a Group or Duo

Start a group for your apartment, upcoming vacation, dinner party, or simply a 1-on-1 tab with your best friend or partner.

Step 2

Add Expenses in Seconds

Type in the amount, tag who was involved, and let Chukta calculate each person's exact share automatically in real time.

Step 3

Hit "Chukta" to Settle Up

When it's time to clear balances, tap Settle. Pay via UPI or your preferred payment method, and mark the ledger Chukta with 1 tap.
