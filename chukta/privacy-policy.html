Privacy Policy

Summary at a glance: Chukta is built on strict data minimalism. We do not sell your personal data, we do not run behavioral advertising networks, and we never hold your bank account passwords or UPI MPINs.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how WLTech ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use the Chukta mobile application, web dashboard, and related services (collectively referred to as the "Service").

By accessing or using Chukta, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please refrain from using our application.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly required to provide you with seamless expense management and settlement tracking:

A. Account Information

  • Contact Details: Your name, email address, or mobile phone number provided during account registration or sign-in.
  • Profile Metadata: Profile picture or display alias you optionally choose to upload.

B. Expense & Ledger Records

  • Transactions: Description of expenses, amounts, timestamps, currencies, and participant splits created within groups or 1-on-1 ledgers.
  • Group Information: Group names, member lists, and group categories (e.g., "Trips", "Apartment", "Dinner").
  • Receipt Attachments (Optional): Images or files of physical bills or receipts that you explicitly choose to upload to an expense.

C. Device & Technical Information

  • Device Telemetry: Device model, operating system version, app version, language preference, and anonymous crash logs used solely for diagnosing bugs and performance regressions.
  • Offline Sync Logs: Local database cache timestamp to reconcile entries across devices when returning online.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

To ensure your financial peace of mind, we enforce strict zero-custody boundaries:

  • No Bank Credentials: We never ask for, view, or store your banking passwords, ATM PINs, OTPs, or UPI MPINs.
  • No Direct Funds Custody: When you tap to settle, payments occur directly between you and your counterparties via verified third-party payment rails (e.g., UPI applications, net banking, cash). We do not hold escrow or customer deposits.
  • No Contact Scraping: We do not upload your entire phonebook to our servers. Contact picker permissions are only utilized locally on-device when you select a friend to invite.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To calculate balances, net settlement debts, and maintain accurate group ledgers.
  • To synchronize expense data across multiple devices within your shared groups.
  • To notify group members when an expense is logged or when a balance has been marked as settled.
  • To deliver push notifications regarding reminders and account security alerts (which you can toggle off anytime).
  • To protect against malicious activity, spam, and fraudulent abuse of our servers.

5. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data. We only share information in the following limited situations:

  • With Other Group Members: Members of a shared group can view the expenses, notes, and split breakdowns logged within that specific group.
  • Trusted Infrastructure Providers: Highly vetted cloud hosting, database, and push notification providers (e.g., ISO/IEC 27001 and SOC 2 certified cloud infrastructure) bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by valid legal process (court order, subpoena, or applicable statutory laws), we may disclose relevant records strictly to the minimum extent demanded by law.

6. Data Security & Storage

Security is foundational to how WLTech builds software. We employ robust defensive safeguards:

  • Transport Layer Security: All communications between your device and our servers use modern TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Encryption at Rest: Database records and media attachments are encrypted using AES-256 standard encryption.
  • Access Control: Internal access to infrastructure is gated behind strict multi-factor authentication, least-privilege policies, and regular audit logs.

7. Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your data only for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide our services. You maintain complete control:

  • You can leave or delete individual groups at any time.
  • You can request complete account deletion directly from the Chukta application settings or by emailing our privacy team. Upon confirmation, your profile and associated personal records are permanently erased from our production databases within 30 days.

8. Your Rights & Controls

Regardless of your geographic location, you enjoy the following privacy rights:

  • Access & Export: Request a digital copy of all personal transaction data associated with your account.
  • Correction: Update or rectify inaccuracies in your profile details at any time.
  • Erasure: Request the total deletion of your personal data.
  • Revocation of Consent: Turn off push notifications, camera permissions, or local storage access through your operating system settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Chukta is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a minor has provided us with personal data, we will take immediate steps to remove such information from our records.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements or regulatory developments. When material updates are made, we will notify you through an in-app notice, email, or by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
